# Banking navigation references

Research for Blubby's bottom-tab experiment. These references help assess separation by product area and direct access to tasks; they do not replace the approved visual moodboard.

## Public screenshots

This directory contains 18 public App Store marketing screenshots, six per bank: `nubank-01.webp` through `nubank-06.webp`, `pagbank-01.webp` through `pagbank-06.webp`, and `inter-01.webp` through `inter-06.webp`.

[sources.json](sources.json) records source pages, image URLs, dimensions, hashes, and collection dates. The pages are [Nubank](https://apps.apple.com/br/app/nubank-conta-cart%C3%A3o-e-mais/id814456780), [PagBank](https://apps.apple.com/br/app/banco-pagbank/id1186059012), and [Inter](https://apps.apple.com/br/app/id839711154).

These are marketing images: they may crop or hide navigation and do not establish which tabs are currently available in the apps. They are analysis references, not assets incorporated into Blubby.

## Official navigation sources

- [Nubank — official video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J1RN7IX1av8), published on 2022-08-10: a historical reference for organizing three tabs, not confirmation of the current menu.
- [PagBank — cards](https://blog.pagbank.com.br/cartoes-pagbank): describes accessing the “Cartões” tab in the bottom menu.
- [Inter — travel experience](https://blog.inter.co/inter-lanca-nova-experiencia-de-viagens-com-ofertas-exclusivas-na-orange-friday/), published on 2025-11-21: describes access through “Todos” in the bottom menu.

These sources document examples of accessing product areas. They neither establish Blubby's navigation nor replace validation of its proposal.

## Adaptation for Blubby

[Alternative B in Figma](https://www.figma.com/design/QYrBUue2AdyrjvJLiMu7F2/blubby?node-id=83-99) adapts only area separation and direct access. Its Portuguese UI labels are:

- “Planejamento”: month and projection.
- “Lançamentos”: income and expenses.
- “Contas”: accounts, wallets, and cards.
- “Ajustes”: settings.

The add action remains separate from the tabs. Pix, investments, shopping, and bank offers do not enter the MVP by association with these references.

This visual proposal has not been approved as final navigation. It has not undergone device or usability testing. The flow and open decisions are in [monthly-planning.md](../../../docs/flows/monthly-planning.md).
