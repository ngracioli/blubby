## Alternative B — bottom-tab experiment

[Alternative B in Figma](https://www.figma.com/design/QYrBUue2AdyrjvJLiMu7F2/blubby?node-id=83-99) tests a bottom tab bar based on [banking navigation references](../references/navigation-banks/README.md). It is a visual proposal under review, not approved final navigation. The original wireframe remains above the alternative in the file.

Both states retain 393 × 852 units: a month with records (`83:105`) and first use (`83:142`). Previews are stored alongside the originals:

- [monthly-planning-navigation-current.png](monthly-planning-navigation-current.png).
- [monthly-planning-navigation-first-use.png](monthly-planning-navigation-first-use.png).
- [monthly-planning-navigation-review.png](monthly-planning-navigation-review.png).

Proposed Portuguese tab labels are “Planejamento” (month and projection), “Lançamentos” (income and expenses), “Contas” (accounts, wallets, and cards), and “Ajustes” (settings). Each combines an icon and name, with an indicator and color for the active state and a 98.25 × 64-unit touch target. Adding remains a separate fixed action, not a fifth tab.

The bar reduces the visible content area from 706 to 638 units, with a scrolling list area. Financial data remains identical to the original wireframe. The alternative is static, with unconnected destinations and no usability or device testing.

## Content and visual direction

Data is fictional. The current balance of R$ 4,280, plus R$ 1,000 in pending income, minus R$ 650 in pending account expenses and R$ 1,800 in unpaid card bills, produces a projected balance of R$ 2,830.

Monthly totals are R$ 6,000 in income, R$ 1,450 in account expenses, and R$ 1,800 in card bills. Details distinguish outstanding amounts used in the projection.

The design uses Manrope and Lucide, reusing project tokens and components. Its initial hierarchy presents the selected month, prominent current balance, secondary projected balance, income/account expenses/card bills, and recent activity with an add action.

## Review status

Wireframes are static, with unconnected destinations. They reserve system areas without reproducing native bars; global navigation remains pending, with alternative B under review. They await user review and have not been tested on devices or with enlarged text.
